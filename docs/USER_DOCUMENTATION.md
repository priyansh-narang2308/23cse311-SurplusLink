## Introduction
SurplusLink connects food donors (like restaurants and hotels) with NGOs and local volunteers to ensure that surplus food reaches those in need before it spoils. Our mission is to eliminate food waste and fight hunger through intelligent logistics and community action.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Registration & Login
- **Sign Up**: Click on the 'Sign Up' button on the landing page. Choose your role: **Donor**, **NGO**, or **Volunteer**.
- **Role-Specific Details**:
    - **Donors**: Provide business name and location.
    - **NGOs**: Provide organization name, mission focus, and upload verification documents (permits, tax IDs).
    - **Volunteers**: Provide personal details and vehicle information (Cycle, Bike, Car, Van).
- **Verification**: NGOs and Volunteers must be approved by an Admin before they can fully participate in missions.
- **Security**: We use secure OTP (One-Time Password) and Google Social Login for a seamless and safe experience.

##  Donor Guide
As a Donor, you are the source of the surplus food. Your goal is to list food quickly and accurately.

### 1. Creating a Donation
- Click **"Post New Donation"** on your dashboard.
- **Details Needed**:
    - **Food Item Name**: e.g., "Mixed Vegetable Biryani".
    - **Quantity**: Number of meals or weight.
    - **Food Type**: Choose Veg, Non-Veg, or Halal.
    - **Allergens**: Mark any common allergens (e.g., Nuts, Dairy).
    - **Expiry Time**: Specify when the food will no longer be safe to consume.
- **Photos**: Upload a clear photo of the food to help NGOs verify the quality.
- **Pickup Scheduling**: Specify a time window for the volunteer to pick up the food.

### 2. Monitoring Impact
- Your **Donor Dashboard** shows your total meals saved, CO2 emissions avoided, and community trust score.
- View your **Donation History** to track which NGOs received your food and see their feedback.

## NGO Guide
NGOs are the beneficiaries who redistribute the food to those in need.

### 1. Finding Food (The Smart Feed)
- Access the **Smart Feed** to see available donations nearby.
- **Filters**: Sort by food type, distance, or urgency (time to expiry).
- **Map View**: Use the integrated map to visualize all active donations in your area.

### 2. Managing Capacity
- Set your **Daily Intake Limits** in your profile settings.
- Specify your **Storage Types** (Cold, Frozen, Dry) so the system only matches you with suitable food.

### 3. Claiming & Verification
- **Claim**: Click "Claim" on a donation card to initiate the pickup process.
- **Rejection**: If a donation arrives and is found to be unsafe or of poor quality, use the "Reject" button with a mandatory reason. This helps us maintain system-wide safety standards.

## Volunteer Guide
Volunteers are the vital link that moves food from Donors to NGOs.

### 1. Managing Availability
- Use the **Online/Offline** toggle on your dashboard.
- Only when you are "Online" will you receive notifications for nearby delivery missions.

### 2. Accepting Missions
- When a mission is available, you'll see a **Task Card** with:
    - **Route Preview**: Map view of pickup and drop-off points.
    - **ETA**: Estimated time for the entire delivery.
    - **Cargo Details**: What is being transported and any special handling rules.
- Click **"Accept Mission"** to lock the task to yourself.

### 3. Delivery Execution (On-Field)
- **Confirm Pickup**: Once you arrive at the Donor's location and receive the food, click "Confirm Pickup." You will be asked to upload a photo of the food at handover.
- **Optimized Routing**: Use the in-app Google Maps integration for the fastest route, avoiding traffic delays.
- **Confirm Delivery**: Upon reaching the NGO, click "Confirm Delivery." The NGO will provide a digital signature or you'll take a final photo to complete the mission.

### 4. Offline Support
- If you lose internet connectivity while on the road, don't worry! You can still view mission addresses. Any delivery actions you take (like clicking "Confirm") will be saved and automatically uploaded once you're back online.

## Admin Guide
Admins oversee the entire ecosystem to ensure safety, fairness, and efficiency.

### 1. User Verification (KYC Hub)
- Review pending NGO and Volunteer applications.
- Examine uploaded documents and vehicle specs before clicking **Approve** or **Reject**.

### 2. Live Logistics Monitoring
- View the **Active Missions** dashboard to see all deliveries happening in real-time.
- **Intervention**: If a mission is stalled or a volunteer reports an issue, you can manually reassign the task or cancel it to prevent food spoilage.

### 3. Safety & Governance
- **Safety Rules**: Update maximum transit times and storage requirements for different food categories.
- **Audit Logs**: Access immutable logs of every critical action (who approved who, why a mission was cancelled, etc.) for full transparency.

## Trust & Safety

### 1. Trust Scores
Every user (Donor, NGO, Volunteer) has a **Trust Score** (starting at 5.0).
- **Increase**: Successful deliveries, positive feedback, and timely actions.
- **Decrease**: Consecutive cancellations, reported violations, or failed safety audits.

### 2. Safety Window
The system automatically prevents any pickups that would occur within 30 minutes of food expiry. This "Safety Window" ensures that the NGOs have enough time to redistribute the food while it is still fresh.
